## Rollback and stop conditions

Stop if baseline or schema differs from the plan, backups cannot be restored, any role sees unauthorized data, old clients break without a reviewed mitigation, concurrent/replay checks fail, a secret is exposed, or host isolation is missing. Do not blindly restore permissive policies or revert to an old AI/order function: that can reintroduce disclosure, bypass rate limits, or expose receipt data. First disable the affected endpoint or put an equivalent access control in place; then use a reviewed forward migration and coordinated app/function rollback based on the verified schema backup. Rehearse on staging and preserve data.

A separately approved production release requires the above results, remediation of failures, a security and operations sign-off, deployment/rollback ownership and another explicit authorization. Nothing in this checklist establishes that approval.
