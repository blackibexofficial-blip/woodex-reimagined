How the counts work

A product is filed at its leaf — “Executive Tables”, not “Office Tables”. The parent's number is its own products plus everything beneath it, so a section that holds nothing directly still shows what it contains.

Coming in a later slice

Ten commerce screens are routed, permissioned and in the sidebar. They open onto this page in the real admin too — honest about status rather than empty.

Attributes · Invoices · Payments · Refunds · Promotions · Shipping & tax · Customers · Reports · Settings

Already underneath: the catalog tables, the money calculator, checkout services and the media pipeline. What is missing is the screen.

  • Attributes: the option lists a variant is built from

  • Payments: verify a bank transfer against an order
  • Reports: sales by day, by category, by series
